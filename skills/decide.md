When to use: the team just chose something, or you need a written decision.

Inputs: the decision text after the command, plus recent history.

Steps:
1. State the decision in one sentence.
2. List alternatives that were considered, if any.
3. Owner, date (today), and remaining open questions.
4. remember the decision (channel scope) so it sticks.
5. @ledger if they are in the room and should record it too.

Output: Decision / Why / Owner / Open questions.

Approval: none to write it down. request_approval before announcing it outside this workspace.
